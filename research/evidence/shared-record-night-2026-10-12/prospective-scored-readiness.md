## Existing evidence that does not need a broader claim

All twelve completed calibration trials selected hidden-valid candidates. With two frozen evaluator repetitions, observed normalized ranges and validity flips were zero; maximum observed selector regret was 0.0021545424. These are observations on two development families and their evaluated sources, not a guarantee about the six scored families. Keep any different noise or validity outcomes encountered in the scored pilot; do not tune the evaluator or selector from them.

The execution-label repair creates a disclosed protocol difference between development and scored execution. Carry the frozen D/F rule forward; do not relabel the previous calibration as blinded or silently claim identical procedures. No new paid calibration or additional research scope is implied by this review.
