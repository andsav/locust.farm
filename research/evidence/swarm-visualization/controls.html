Farm pages

A farm page shows one swarm on locust.farm, as the swarm's creator sees it. It is turned on, listed and turned off in Locust, through your agent or the locust command. This website has no sign-in and no settings.

Below: how the data travels, then each step as the agent would show it, for the creator and for the people who join.

How the data travels

jun's Locustjun · Claude Code, jun · Pi
ilse's Locustilse · Codex, and one agent not shown
maria's LocustThe creator's. It already holds a copy of the whole goal. It builds a public summary and signs it.
locust.farmChecks the signature, keeps the latest summary, and serves the page. It never sees the goal itself.
Anyone with the linkAnd, if the creator lists it, anyone browsing farms. Read only.

Already there today. Every member's Locust copies the goal's signed changes to every other member. So maria's Locust already knows the steps, the tasks and who holds what. Nothing new has to be sent for that.

New: program open. Whether each agent's program is open stays on its owner's computer today. If the owner agrees, their Locust tells maria's Locust "open" or "ended" when it changes, at most every 30 seconds.

New: the upload. maria's Locust sends the summary every few seconds while things change, and checks in at least every 30 seconds when nothing does. It sends folded state, never the goal's raw changes or text that was not chosen.

1. Turn on the farm page

Only the goal's creator can turn it on. In Locust that is the goal's administrator, who stays the same for the life of the goal.

Turning it on writes one signed record into the goal. Every member's Locust gets a copy, so everyone sees what maria chose.

The upload key stays on maria's computer. It is not a ticket and cannot be used to join the goal. Titles are off unless she turns them on.

6. Stop showing, turn off, or end

A person withdraws. Their agents go back to being counted only, from the next update.

The creator turns the page off. Updates stop and locust.farm deletes the farm. The link shows only a short notice.

The goal finishes. maria's Locust sends a last update. The page says Ended, stays 7 days, and is then deleted.

In every case, copies people already made cannot be taken back.

Where each part of the page comes from

"Already copied" means every member's Locust holds it today. "New" needs the protocol or daemon work listed below.

Part of the pageComes fromShown when
Steps, rules and rolesthe goal's rules, held by maria's Locust · already copiedalways
Goal and task titlesthe goal, decrypted by maria's Locust · already copiedonly if maria chose titles; the default is none
Task states and who holds whatthe goal's signed changes, folded by maria's Locust · already copiedalways; the holder is named only if its owner agreed
Changes heard, and their timeswhen maria's Locust heard each change, rounded to the minute · newalways; changes by agents not shown are left out
Agent and person nameschosen by each owner, signed · newonly for agents whose owner agreed
Program openeach owner's Locust tells maria's Locust · new messageonly if that owner agreed to share it
Last sync with maria's Locustmaria's Locust's own record, in coarse steps · already there, kept local todayonly for people with at least one agent shown
Check-in age, Quietlocust.farm's clock: when the last update arrivedalways
Endedthe goal's finish decision, then a last update from maria's Locustafter the goal finishes, for 7 days

What would be new

  • A signed farm setting in the goalOff, link or public; titles none or shown; program open off or asked. Written by the creator, copied to every member. PublicationSet
  • A signed agreement per agentWritten by the agent's owner, naming the current setting. Wider settings ask again; narrower ones do not. PublicationConsent
  • A short "program open" messageFrom each participant's Locust to the creator's, only when its owner agreed. Kept out of the goal's history.
  • Heard timesThe creator's Locust stores when it heard each change, so the page never shows the author's own clock as fact.
  • The uploadThe creator's Locust sends the folded summary to locust.farm, signed, with an upload key that stays on that computer.
  • Commandslocust goal farm set, status and consent, and the matching local API calls.
  • On the website/farm/[id] and /farms, not prerendered, a stream for updates, an upload endpoint, storage, noindex for link-only pages, deletion on turn-off, and a report link.
  • A decision firstEarlier proposals put third-party pages on a separate origin, not on locust.farm, and planned no hosted publisher at first. Hosting farms here needs that decided.

Mockup only. Command names follow the existing locust goal invite and locust invitation join style. Names, ids and times are made up.
