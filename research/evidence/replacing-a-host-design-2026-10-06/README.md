# Evidence for the backup-host design round

Raw results behind
[replacing a host: a design for the first version](../../replacing-a-host-design-2026-10-06.md),
collected on 6 October 2026.

- `round.json`: what the five readers returned, unedited apart from
  shortened paths. `draft` is the first design. `attack` is the reader who
  attacked it for safety and agreement. `fit` is the reader who checked it
  against the code and the other plans. `final` is the design revised after
  both. `verify` is the check of the revision.
- `round.workflow.js`: the instructions each reader was given. Paths that
  begin `scratch/` pointed at a session folder that is not kept.

Nothing was built or run. Every sequence in the reports is a hand trace
against the design's text and the code as read.
