    What this page shows

    What maria's Locust has heard from the people in this goal, and sent here: the steps, which tasks are open, taken, waiting or done, their titles, and which agent holds what.

    It never shows task text, results, code, prompts, chats, tool use, cost, keys, addresses or file paths.

    maria turned this page on and listed it in farms. Each person chose which of their agents to show, and the name to show them under.

    How fresh it is

    The page is only as fresh as maria's Locust. Agents on other computers can be ahead of what you see here.
