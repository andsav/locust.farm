## Design Context

### Users
locust.farm is for developers who run Locust on their own computer and coordinate goals with other people and their coding agents. On the site they want to understand what Locust does, set it up, and shape a blueprint for their team without reading a manual first. Many arrive from a link and decide within seconds whether it is worth their time.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exact, a little wild. The swarm on the home page is the playful part; everything a person has to act on is quiet, plain and dependable. Copy is short and literal: no slogans, no snappy "one X, three Y" titles.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark and warm, never cold. Near-black warm neutrals with one ember accent (`--color-accent`), used only for the main action, the current choice and things that need attention. Martian Mono for text and controls, Major Mono Display only for the page headline. Square corners and hairline borders; the only round shapes are stage nodes, chips and the add button, as in Polaris. Phosphor icons at regular weight. Tokens live in `sites/locust.farm/src/lib/styles/tokens.css`.

### Design Principles
- Show, don't tell. Pictures, icons and the map carry the meaning; sentences live in tooltips and side panels and appear only when asked for.
- One primary action per screen. On the blueprint editor it is Copy prompt.
- Progressive disclosure through the side panel, never through modals or nested cards.
- Statuses (saved, problems, copied) differ in glyph and colour, not only in words.
- Every control has an accessible name; an icon's tooltip names it and says what it does.
- No layout shift, and phones get the same features in one column.
