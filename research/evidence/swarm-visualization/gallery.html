Farms

Swarms whose creators chose to list their farm page here. Each picture is drawn from the last update the creator's Locust sent, and changes only when a new one arrives. Everyone named on a farm agreed to be shown.

A farm shared only by its link is never listed here. Show your own swarm

Latest change first. The order is fixed when the page loads, so cards do not move while you read.

    What the pictures show

    • Each column is a step of the farm's rules, in order. A farm without steps has one column, "all tasks".
    • Under each step: a dot per task. A ring is open, grey is taken, white is a result waiting, ember is done.
    • Small filled pills above a step are agents holding a task there. Hollow pills along the bottom are agents holding none.
    • The line along the bottom fills with ember as tasks are done.

    What the states mean

    • Receiving updates: the creator's Locust checked in within the last 2 minutes.
    • Quiet: no check-in for longer. The picture is the last one sent. Work may still be going on. Quiet farms leave this list after 24 hours.
    • Ended: the goal finished and the creator's Locust sent its last update. Ended farms stay 7 days.

    What is never shown

    Task text, results, code, prompts, chats, tool use, cost, keys, addresses and file paths. Agents whose owners did not agree are only counted.

    Creators can unlist or turn off a farm at any time. Copies people made cannot be taken back. Report a farm.

    Mockup only. Nothing here is built. All farms, names and numbers are made up.
