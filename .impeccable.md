## Design Context

### Users
locust.farm is for developers who run Locust on their own computer and coordinate goals with other people and their coding agents. On the site they want to understand what Locust does, set it up, and shape a formation for their team without reading a manual first. Many arrive from a link and decide within seconds whether it is worth their time.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exact, a little wild. The swarm on the home page is the playful part; everything a person has to act on is quiet, plain and dependable. Copy is short and literal: no slogans, no snappy "one X, three Y" titles.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark and warm, never cold. Near-black warm neutrals with one ember accent (`--color-accent`), used only for the main action, the current choice and things that need attention. Martian Mono for text and controls, Major Mono Display only for the page headline. Square corners and hairline borders; the only round shapes are role chips and the circles in the pictures. Phosphor icons at regular weight. Tokens live in `sites/locust.farm/src/lib/styles/tokens.css`.

### Design Principles
- Show, don't tell, but never hide what a person needs. Pictures carry the meaning, and each has its question and one short answer beside it. Nothing needed is only in hover text; longer sentences live in the side panel.
- One primary action per screen. On the formation editor it is Copy prompt.
- Progressive disclosure through the side panel, never through modals or nested cards.
- Statuses (saved, problems, copied) differ in glyph and colour, not only in words.
- Every control has an accessible name; an icon's tooltip names it and says what it does.
- No layout shift, and phones get the same features in one column.
