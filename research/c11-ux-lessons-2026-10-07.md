# c11: UX lessons for Locust

Date: 2026-10-07. Status: source assessment and proposals, not an accepted
implementation plan or measured usability result.

## Assessment and method

c11 offers useful ideas for keeping concurrent work understandable: route human
attention deliberately, keep identity stable, qualify status by its evidence,
and make it easy to return to the exact thing that needs a decision. These
ideas fit Locust without making it a terminal manager or requiring a coordinator.

Reviewed [c11](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11) at commit
`2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0`, including Swift implementation,
test sources, its agent skill, and repository screenshots. Compared with Locust
at `2b31e6969cf59db2a9376683632b4d6076b88f28`. c11 was not built or launched;
its tests were read, not run. Repository screenshots show captured examples,
not a current live session. No Polaris source comparison was performed.

c11's product boundary is a local workspace containing terminals, browsers and
Markdown panels. Its [philosophy](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/PHILOSOPHY.md)
puts workflow policy above these primitives. Locust instead maintains a signed
shared board with formation rules and local authorization; see
[the current concepts](../docs/guide/concepts.md). Its natural primary objects
remain goals, tasks, attempts, contributions and decisions.

## 1. Treat attention as a separate concern

**Observed in c11.** The Feed separates asks from finished turns. Explicit flags
come before open asks; each group uses oldest-first ordering with stable identity
tie breaks. An explicit flag survives turn completion and bypasses suppression.
Keyboard selection follows the panel UUID when rows move. These behaviors are
implemented in the [Feed projector](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Feed/FeedProjector.swift#L145-L179),
[ordering](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Feed/AttentionOrder.swift#L21-L44),
and [selection](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Feed/FeedQuickViewSelection.swift#L3-L32).
[Projection tests](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/c11Tests/FeedProjectorTests.swift#L390-L423)
cover flags after completion and through suppression.

**Locust already has.** The owner status prints “Waiting for you” and an actionable
command before its goal inventory. Its two waiting kinds concern task allowance
and changing read access. Other pending work, including reviews and new context,
appears in a separate per-goal view. See the
[status and pending renderers](../crates/locust/src/cli/presentation.rs) and
[API types](../crates/locust-proto/src/api.rs).

**Proposal.** Build on that structure with a coherent local view across goals:
what needs this person's decision, what changed since their last visit, and
where to inspect it. Keep agent-owned reviews out of the human action queue
unless escalation or local policy calls for intervention. Each item needs an
explicit resolver, reason, stable target and next eligible action. Keep selection
stable while events arrive; opening a row should not acknowledge or resolve it.

**Adaptation required.** c11's
[suppressed waiting projection](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/AttentionModel.swift#L58-L67)
displays idle. Locust should retain the actual state alongside a notification
preference, for example “Waiting for review · notifications muted.” Routing to a
supervisor only applies when that relationship exists. Remote peers should not
gain unlimited interruption privileges. Queue age also needs a local observation
or explicit distributed ordering rule, rather than trusting peer wall clocks.

## 2. Separate identity, current activity and attention

**Observed in c11.** A panel exposes a title, description, harness/model, status
and separate clocks. The [agent skill](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/skills/c11/SKILL.md)
recommends stable titles and current descriptions; this is a convention, not an
enforced freshness guarantee. Descriptions fall back to useful locations for
non-agent panels. The panel detail builder keeps these fields distinct, and the
description bar adds no height when empty:
[detail model](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/TabSheetDetail.swift#L99-L137),
[description fallback](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/TabSheetDetail.swift#L165-L184),
[bar rendering](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/PanelTitleBarView.swift#L5-L27).

**Proposal.** A compact Locust row could read:

> Ari · reviewer · Claude Code
>
> Checking the parser proposal; waiting for test evidence
>
> Last report 2 minutes ago · open contribution

This is an illustrative design, not current output. Locust already separates
member names, roles and local levels in its
[goal rows](../crates/locust/src/cli/presentation.rs). Add transient context
without replacing those fields. In Locust, a role has authority under formation
rules; an agent-written description must not change or impersonate it. Show
harness/model only when known and useful.

The transferable benefit is lower reorientation cost: the reader can remember
who this is and understand what changed without opening every transcript.

## 3. Put uncertainty beside the status

**Observed in c11.** Agent-reported status and progress have age-based decay.
Expired status can yield to a visibly derived activity value. Historical journal
state is qualified as disconnected/unconfirmed. See
[status projection](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Sidebar/SidebarActivityProjector.swift#L451-L503),
[age thresholds](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/SidebarStalenessSettings.swift#L23-L88),
and [historical-state help](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Sidebar/SidebarActivityProjector.swift#L87-L104).

**Locust already has.** Session rendering distinguishes the adapter's last report,
connection and claims, and explicitly says none proves the client is running.
The [farm view](../sites/locust.farm/src/routes/farm/[id]/+page.svelte) distinguishes
publisher check-in from snapshot update. Preserve these distinctions as the UX
becomes more compact.

**Proposal.** Prefer “Last reported working 3 minutes ago; connection lost” to a
permanent green running dot. Keep unresolved decisions visible across disconnects,
while revalidating any offered action. Treat stale descriptions as stale too:
c11's [metadata guide](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/skills/c11/references/metadata.md)
says descriptions do not decay, leaving freshness to agent discipline. A signed
report establishes who reported it, not whether the work succeeded.

## 4. Preserve the path from interruption to evidence to action

**Observed in c11.** The Feed identifies an exact panel; its guarded answer path
rechecks state before delayed submission. That reply path is limited to eligible
flags and turn completions, not generic approval of permission dialogs. The
[answer implementation](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/Feed/FeedAnswer.swift#L27-L101)
is more useful inspiration than copying an unrestricted reply box.

**Proposal.** Let a Locust attention item open the precise contribution, diff,
review evidence or allowance request. Present the action for that exact version
beside the relevant evidence. Bind actions to existing revision/identity checks;
if the request changes, refresh rather than silently acting on its replacement.
Inspection, acknowledgment, review, integration and updating a local folder must
remain separate. Locust's [apply guide](../docs/guide/apply.md) already defines
these boundaries, and pending review output already links to exact events.

c11's [mailbox guide](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/docs/c11-mailbox-guide.md)
also distinguishes durable messages from terminal input and exposes their
delivery history. Borrow that inspectability: sent, delivered, acknowledged and
acted on are different facts. Mail arriving in an inbox is not task completion.

Its [focus policy](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/TerminalController.swift#L434-L460)
also limits which commands may change focus. The corresponding Locust principle
is straightforward: an incoming peer event can update the queue without moving
the reader away from their current work or disturbing a reply in progress.

## 5. Keep a compact overview with an explicit way to expand it

**Observed in repository captures.** The
[drawer](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/design/tab-bar-qol/validation/round-five/dark-drawer820.png)
aligns names, agent identity, status and clocks in scan-friendly columns; the
[rail](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/design/tab-bar-qol/validation/round-five/dark-rail-large.png)
keeps a vertical overview beside the selected content. These are alternative
presentations of the same panels. The implemented
[rail-tip policy](https://github.com/Stage-11-Agentics/c11/blob/2a8591d95113cf0c5b07ae6924627f457f748cb0/Sources/PanelRailTipPolicy.swift#L70-L87)
offers the denser mode after recurring overflow, with undo and dismissal.

**Proposal.** A private Locust view could show compact goal/task rows with a
selected detail pane. At larger scale, preserve location and reveal more detail
on demand. Use textual reasons alongside color, and omit unknown metrics rather
than inventing progress. The terminal split hierarchy and large collection of
clocks are less useful to Locust than stable rows and an easy path to evidence.
Do not copy c11's expert-density preference as a requirement for first contact.

## Smallest useful next experiment

Prototype a private, read-only attention view over existing status, pending work,
session reports and contribution inspection. It could begin in the CLI; this
assessment does not justify a new native app. Use explicit fixtures for several
simultaneous goals, a disconnected participant, a completed attempt awaiting
review, an obsolete request, and a locally denied task.

Test whether a person can identify the next decision and its resolver, open the
right evidence, distinguish fresh from historical state, and return to previous
work. Check that arrivals do not change the selected target, and merely looking
does not acknowledge anything. Compare with the existing CLI workflow; no claim
of improved usability is warranted until that comparison is performed.

Keep this separate from public farm pages. Their
[publication contract](../docs/guide/farm-publication.md) intentionally excludes
task text, results, code and local paths. Keep each person's local authority
intact, and keep coordination selectable through formation rules. c11's main
lesson for Locust is to reduce the human effort of understanding concurrent work.
